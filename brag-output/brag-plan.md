# DoubleTick: Customer Stories (plan)

## Timing conflict and how it was resolved
- The brief targets 45–60s, with Scene 05 ending at 00:50. The supplied voiceover is **91.4s** long.
- The VO is tightly paced: the longest pause is 0.56s. Getting to ~50s would mean cutting whole script sentences or speeding the narration up 1.8×. The brief forbids changing the script wording or replacing the narrator, so neither is acceptable.
- **Resolution:** the five-scene hierarchy stays exactly as written, but every scene is re-timed to the VO's real sentence boundaries, found with silence detection and mapped to the script. Total runtime is **94.5s**: 91.4s of VO plus a 3.1s final brand hold.
- If a true 45–60s cut is needed, the VO has to be re-recorded without the three "See how / Discover how / Explore how…" sentences (≈20s) and the opening questions of scenes 3–4 (≈6s). That would give ≈62s with no other changes.

## Angle
Three customers, three problems, each solved inside the same DoubleTick visual system. Each story follows the same order: challenge → fix → capability → number. Shared-element hand-offs tie the stories together.
