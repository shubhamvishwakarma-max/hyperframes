# DoubleTick AI Voice — 10s square brag

**What it is:** An AI voice agent that answers and makes customer calls for a business, on WhatsApp calls and regular phone numbers.
**For:** Sales and support teams already running on DoubleTick.
**Sets it apart:** It's one agent for WhatsApp calls and PSTN numbers. It speaks Hindi, English, or a mix, and logs every call to the CRM.
**Visual hook:** A phone rings, and before a human can reach it, a glowing voice orb answers.
**Share caption:** The call came in at 2am. The AI picked up.

## Angle
"Every call gets answered." Show the agent at work instead of describing it: a ring, an answer, a live Hinglish exchange, a call logged.

## Tone
`default`, pushed toward dynamic: punchy, kinetic type, spring motion, quick soft transitions.

## Visual identity
- Deep ink background `#070B14` with a soft radial glow
- DoubleTick green `#19C37D` for brand and "answered" moments, violet `#8B7CFF` for the AI orb
- Inter Display / Inter, heavy weights for headlines

## Storyboard (10.0s, 30fps, 1080×1080)
| # | Time | Scene | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–2.3 | Hook: ring | An incoming-call card shakes in ("Incoming WhatsApp call"). Ring pulses. Kinetic headline: **"Your customer is calling."** |
| 2 | 2.3–5.2 | Answer and talk | The card morphs into the AI orb, and a live waveform plays. Chat-style transcript bubbles pop in: "Namaste! How can I help?" / "Order kab tak aayega?" / "Aaj dispatch ho raha hai ✓". A chip row reads Hindi · English · Mixed. |
| 3 | 5.2–7.6 | One agent, every line | The WhatsApp and Phone icons fly into the orb. **"WhatsApp calls + phone calls. One AI agent."** A CRM card then slides up: Transcript ✓ Outcome ✓ Next step ✓ |
| 4 | 7.6–10.0 | Outro | **DoubleTick AI Voice**, **"Answers every call."**, doubletick.io |

## Sound
A 120 BPM pulse in A minor: a soft sub kick, an airy Am–F–C–G pad, a ring motif played as pad notes (A–E), a filtered whoosh under each cut, and a soft chime on the final logo, in the same key.
