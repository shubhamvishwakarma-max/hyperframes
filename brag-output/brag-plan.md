# La Vida Clinic × DoubleTick — case-study ad plan

**Format:** 1080×1080, 30 fps, H.264, ~42.5 s (the brief asks for 35–40 s; the supplied script read at a natural pace runs 34 s of speech, so the cut lands at ~42 s with breathing room).
**Tone:** polished — premium SaaS case study, calm, trustworthy, WhatsApp-first.

**Angle:** Growth didn't break La Vida. Fragmentation did. Six clinics' worth of scattered patient chats get gathered by one green line into one WhatsApp CRM, and the proof points land one at a time.

**Hook (0–2 s):** a coral-tinted frame with six "Clinic 0X" cards whose unread badges keep climbing, and above them in large serif type: "6 locations. Too many patient conversations to track clearly." You get the problem on mute before any word is spoken.

**Highlights:** 30,000+ patient leads in one CRM → 1,500–2,000 automated confirmations a month → lost leads re-engaged.
**Punchline:** 11% reconversion from lost opportunities, then "Built for growing clinics in the UAE" and the CTA.

**Visual identity (taken from doubletick.io):** cream #F4F3EC / warm white, ink #131316, DoubleTick green #27B578, deep green #007A5A, mint #E5FFEF, grey #70707B. The coral tint appears only in the problem opening. Hedvig Letters Serif for headlines and KPIs, Geist for UI, labels, subtitles and the CTA. The real DoubleTick logo SVG comes from the site, and the La Vida logo was supplied by the user.

## Sound
- The VO is a neural TTS voice (en-US Ava). The ElevenLabs workspace had no credits left.
- The score is synthesised at 112 BPM in D. A sparse B-minor pulse plays under the problem, a riser carries the green sweep, then I–V–vi–IV in D major with a soft four-on-the-floor.
- The SFX are tuned to the key: chat pops, UI snaps, the confirm chime, the KPI impact and the CTA resolve.
- The music ducks under the VO with a sidechain compressor.
