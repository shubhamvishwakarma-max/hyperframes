## Intent

Premium B2B SaaS brand ad for DoubleTick (WhatsApp platform). One continuous story:
numbers → one governed inbox → personalise → capture lead → automate → CRM → calls/groups →
tracking → AI agents (chat + voice, "live!") → customer logos → MakeMyTrip results → end card.
Enterprise, restrained, editorial. Each visual beat readable in ~1s.

## Assets

- assets/audio/voiceover.mp3 — supplied ElevenLabs VO (master track, verbatim, 38.7s).
- assets/brand/logo-doubletick.png — supplied DoubleTick logo; top-centre throughout.
- assets/brand/logo-*.png — supplied customer logos (Nissan, Navi, Shaadi.com, Justdial, Sobha, MakeMyTrip), cropped, not recoloured.
- assets/ui/src-strip.webp — supplied product UI strip (2000×343). Too small to show directly at 1080p, so each screen is rebuilt in HTML from its layout and copy; photos (ad photo, catalogue photo, AI agent avatar) are cropped from it.

## Customizations

- End card: "Scale Every Conversation. Stay in Control."
